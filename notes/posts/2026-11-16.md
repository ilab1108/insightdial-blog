## LinkedIn post
A contractor who is closed shouldn't get your call.

Home services calls look simple: a homeowner phones, a company answers. But the rules behind that call decide whether anyone gets paid fairly.

One practical habit for pay-per-call operators: collect four things from every buyer before sending traffic. Opening hours, a daily cap, the states they serve, and a number that reaches someone who can book the job.

With those in place you can order buyers in a waterfall. If the first is closed or full, the call falls through to the next instead of being wasted. Add a written definition of a billable call (minimum duration, repeat-caller rule, allowed sources) and most disputes never start.

Then track each traffic source with its own number, so you can see which partner sent which call and pause the weak ones.

Compliance rules vary, so check your obligations with your own counsel.
