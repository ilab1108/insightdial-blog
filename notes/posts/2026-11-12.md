## LinkedIn post

A legal call is only worth its payout if both sides agree on what "a call" means.

Law firms pay more per call than many other buyers. That makes every unclear rule expensive, for the firm and for whoever sends the calls.

Before sending a single live call to a law firm, write down:

- Which practice area the calls are for.
- Which states the firm can take cases in.
- How many seconds a call must last to be billable.
- Intake hours and a daily cap.
- What happens when the same person calls twice.

Then make the software enforce it. A pre-transfer check that confirms the firm is open and under its cap stops calls ringing an empty office. State rules stop calls the firm can never sign. Recordings or transcripts settle disputes with facts instead of memory.

Agree the rules first, and the invoices stop being arguments.
