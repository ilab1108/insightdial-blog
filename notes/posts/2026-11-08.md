# LinkedIn post - 2026-11-08

Clicks leave a trail. Phone calls don't, unless you build one.

If you promote pay-per-call offers, here is one habit that saves arguments later: give every traffic source its own tracking number.

When a visitor reads your page and dials, the number they dial should tell you where they came from. Then you can compare sources by calls, not just clicks, and you can show a buyer exactly which source sent which call.

A few things worth settling before live traffic starts:

- Which calls are payable: minimum length, location, repeat callers.
- Which numbers are blocked.
- Where you can see your own calls and earnings.
- How quality is shown per source, so a weak placement gets fixed early.

Ten minutes of test calls beats a week of calls nobody can credit.
