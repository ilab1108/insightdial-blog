# LinkedIn post - 2026-11-11

Your partners judge your network by one screen: the one they log in to.

If you run a pay-per-call network, every affiliate and every buyer is asking the same question in a different way: "Was I treated fairly?"

Affiliates want to see each call they sent, how long it lasted and what it earned. Buyers want to see each call they paid for and why it counted. When the answers live in spreadsheets you email around, every payout becomes a conversation.

A branded portal changes that. A few things worth getting right before you invite anyone:

- Each partner sees only their own calls and rates.
- The call count matches across your dashboard, the affiliate view and the buyer view.
- Payable-call rules (minimum length, states, repeat callers) are written down first.
- Every source has its own tracking number.
- Test calls show up correctly on both sides.

Shared records turn disputes into quick checks.
