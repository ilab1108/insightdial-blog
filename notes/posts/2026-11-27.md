## LinkedIn post

A missed call from a paid ad is a click you already paid for.

Most businesses know they miss some calls. Few know which campaign sends the calls that go unanswered.

One practical fix: give every campaign its own tracking number, then compare answered and unanswered calls by source and by hour. The pattern usually shows up fast: ads running after closing time, one team stretched at peak, or a number that rings a phone nobody checks.

Then fix the routing:
- Set hours per destination
- Add a fall-back so a ring never dead-ends
- Send alerts to the channel your team already watches

Business hours and holidays differ by country, so check them for every market you advertise in.
