## LinkedIn post
Running pay per call outside one country? Your first problem won't be traffic.

It will be definitions.

A buyer in Singapore, a buyer in Canada and a publisher in the UK are rarely online at the same time, format numbers the same way, or agree on what a "billable call" is.

Three things that save disputes before you go international:
1. Set buyer hours and daily caps in each buyer's own time zone.
2. Write down the billable-call definition (minimum duration, unique caller window) and share it with everyone.
3. Target each campaign to the country or state the buyer actually serves.

And check recording, consent and do-not-call rules with your own counsel for every market. They differ by country.
