## LinkedIn post

Your property portal says it sent 200 enquiries. How many of them actually called?

Real estate buyers, renters and sellers often prefer to pick up the phone, and that is exactly where most reporting goes blank. The portal, the social ad and the board outside the property all look the same once the phone rings.

One practical fix: give every channel its own tracking number. Portal listings, each ad campaign, printed brochures and boards each get a number, and website visitors see a number tied to how they arrived. Then compare calls by source, and listen to a sample of recordings before you move budget.

It works the same whether you sell homes in London, Dubai, Sydney or Toronto. Just remember that call recording rules differ by country, so check your obligations first.
