## LinkedIn post

Your form leads probably say "direct" more often than they should.

A visitor clicks an ad on Monday, comes back by typing your address on Thursday, then fills in the form. Most setups credit the last visit, so the ad that did the work gets nothing.

One practical fix: give every campaign its own tracking link, record the source on the first visit, and store it with the lead. Then compare form leads against calls and clicks, because many customers phone instead of filling in a form, and form-only reports make call-driven campaigns look weak.

Rules on cookies and consent differ by country, so check your obligations with your own counsel.
