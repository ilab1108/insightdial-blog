## LinkedIn post

Your first paying calls shouldn't be your test calls.

Before sending live traffic on a pay-per-call campaign, run through a short checklist:

- Write down what counts as a billable call (minimum duration, repeat callers).
- Confirm buyer hours, daily caps and geography.
- Set a fall-back route so no call is dropped when every buyer says no.
- Give each source its own tracking number and agree payout rates up front.
- Place a test call on every number, including one outside buyer hours.

Most launch problems are configuration mistakes, and they are cheap to catch before real callers dial in.
