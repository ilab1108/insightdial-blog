# LinkedIn post - 2026-11-06

The most expensive test call is the one a real customer makes for you.

Before a pay-per-call campaign goes live, one habit saves a lot of arguments: write down what a payable call is, then check your setup against that sentence.

A quick version:
- What is the minimum call length?
- Which states or countries count?
- What hours and daily caps does the buyer want?
- Which numbers and repeat callers are excluded?
- Does each traffic source have its own tracking number and payout rate?

If the answer to any of those lives only in someone's head or in a chat thread, it will get applied differently by different people. Put it in the campaign settings instead.

Then call every tracking number yourself. Check the call lands with the right buyer, is credited to the right source, and shows up in your records. It takes a few minutes, and it's far cheaper than finding the problem after a day of live traffic.
