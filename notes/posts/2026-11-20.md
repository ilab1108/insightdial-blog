Running pay-per-call for several clients? Keep every client in its own campaign.

Agencies get into trouble when client rules live in emails and spreadsheets. One client closes at 5pm, another caps at 30 calls a day, a third only serves two states. If those rules aren't enforced in routing, a call goes to the wrong place and a report gets disputed.

A simple routine:
1. One campaign per client, with its own tracking numbers.
2. Write down what a billable call is (minimum duration, duplicate rules).
3. Set hours, daily caps, state rules and fall-back before traffic starts.
4. Place test calls end to end.
5. Give clients and partners their own portal view so they can check the numbers themselves.
