## LinkedIn post
When a buyer says "we never got that call," what can you show them?

Many pay-per-call disputes come down to one missing thing: a record of what was sent, and when.

A practical fix is to treat webhooks as part of your campaign setup, not an afterthought:

- Give each webhook one job (CRM record, reporting sheet, team alert)
- Verify the signature on every message
- Ignore repeats by storing call IDs
- Keep a delivery log so you can answer disputes quickly

When every system holds the same call record, there is far less to argue about.
