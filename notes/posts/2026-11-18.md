## LinkedIn post
Insurance calls get disputed over one thing more than any other: nobody wrote down what a billable call is.

Before you send a single Medicare, auto or final expense call to a buyer, agree on five things in writing:

- Minimum call duration
- Where calls can come from (countries or states)
- Buyer hours and daily caps
- Whether a repeat caller counts as new
- The payout and how long either side has to raise a dispute

Then give every traffic source its own tracked number, so you can see which source sends calls that last and which sends short ones.

Clear terms plus one shared call record turn most "that call shouldn't count" arguments into a quick lookup.

Compliance (recording, consent, do-not-call) varies, so check your obligations with your own counsel before launching.
